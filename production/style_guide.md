# Oh Betty Jaletti — Production Style Guide

Visual editing rules for automated content creation in BettyOS.

## Editorial posture

Edit with restraint. The final result should feel human, literary, and refined — never template-made, never trend-chasing.

## Opening

- Begin on real footage immediately.
- Do not open on a full black frame or black title card.
- If a fade is used, keep it very short and soft (≤ 0.25 seconds).

## Text

- Prefer minimal text.
- No opaque black text boxes by default.
- Soft shadow or a barely-there wash may be used only when needed for readability.
- Keep copy short; let the image carry the mood.

## Composition safety

- Protect the book, hand, and product from text overlap.
- Keep platform interface safe zones clear (especially the bottom).
- Place hooks in the upper third; keep footers and CTAs in the lower safe area without crowding the subject.

## Motion and effects

- No decorative effects.
- No trendy transitions.
- Default to simple cuts and subtle fades only.

## Brand feel

Warm, quiet, intentional. Upper West Side literary apartment energy with modern editorial restraint — not cottagecore, not staged, not obviously automated.
