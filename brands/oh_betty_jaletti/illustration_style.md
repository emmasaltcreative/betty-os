## North star

A timeless mid-century fashion illustration viewed through a modern New York
literary atelier. The rendering should read as an illustrator commissioned for a
sophisticated literary fashion publication — **not** a vintage advertising
parody.

- Delicate but confident dark ink linework.
- Slightly expressive doe eyes; painterly, hand-illustrated shading.
- Warm ivory stock with restrained, tactile grain.
- Palette: dark navy, jade/olive, coffee brown, espresso, subtle gold.
- Generous negative space. Real objects, real gestures.

## Calibration against earlier drafts

Reduce orange/sepia warmth and heavy aged-paper effects so assets sit
comfortably beside contemporary ohbettyjaletti.com product photography. Aim
cool-neutral, not monochrome sepia.

Illustrations are ordinarily **secondary** PDP storytelling media. Product hero
photography and illustration complement each other; they do not compete.

## Prohibited

- Plastic skin; generic beige "AI luxury" aesthetics.
- Cartoon eyes; repetitive near-identical faces.
- Floating props; heavy floral clutter.
- Gibberish lettering; fake or re-drawn logos.
- Any model-generated substitute for an official mark.

## Source of truth

The approved *A Woman Remembered* botanical Betty collage is the strongest
visual source of truth for style and composition. It is labelled `style_only`
and `composition_reference` — it does **not** prove product facts it cannot
show.
