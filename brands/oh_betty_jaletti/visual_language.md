# Visual Language — Oh Betty Jaletti

Visual operating rules for planning, selecting assets, and judging whether imagery feels on-brand. Render-time defaults live in the Production Brain (`production/`); this file states brand intent.

## Aesthetic ratios (approved)

Prefer natively shot frames in these ratios:

| Ratio | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **9:16** | Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories |
| **4:5** | Instagram feed / Pinterest-friendly vertical |
| **2:3** | Editorial stills / Pinterest |
| **1:1** | Detail close-ups, texture, flame / material studies |

Do not stretch or invent cinematic ultrawide looks for social. Crop with care; protect subject and safe zones.

## Lighting

- Soft natural window light (afternoon preferred)
- Warm falloff and gentle shadow; slight underexposure reads more lived-in than overlit
- Lamps only if they add quiet warmth at a low level
- Avoid harsh overheads, beauty-ring fill, and flat even “catalog” light

## Composition

- Asymmetry over dead-center (except intentional texture close-ups)
- Negative space is editorial, not empty — leave room for quiet and for text in safe zones
- Protect book, hand, and product from text overlap
- Hooks in the upper third; footers / CTAs in the lower safe area
- Objects earn their place; remove rather than rearrange endlessly
- Hands and rings are welcome when natural; do not force lifestyle posing

## Palette direction

No fixed hex system is locked in BettyOS yet. Direction:

- Warm neutrals, paper, wood, soft textile, late-day light
- White / near-white overlay type at high opacity with soft shadow for readability
- Avoid neon accents, purple lifestyle gradients, heavy color grading, and high-saturation “aesthetic filters”

## Typography character

- Literary serif energy (Production Brain uses Georgia / Times New Roman for overlays)
- Minimal on-screen copy; short lines; image carries mood
- No opaque black text boxes by default; no black title cards as openers
- Centered, restrained hierarchy — never loud display gimmicks

## Motion and edit feel

- Unhurried pacing; simple cuts and subtle fades (≤ ~0.25s)
- Begin on real footage immediately
- No decorative effects or trendy transitions
- Warm, quiet, intentional — Upper West Side literary apartment with modern editorial restraint

## What feels too AI-generated

Reject or restyle when imagery shows:

- Plastic skin, perfect symmetry, or uncanny hands/books
- Generic “stock cozy” prompts: floating dust motes, over-perfect rays, identical marble flats
- Over-smoothed textures, fake film grain layered on already soft footage
- Template layouts, buzzword overlays, or collage boards that look automated
- Anything that feels staged for a model rather than observed in a real room

## What feels too cottagecore

Reject or restyle when imagery shows:

- Basket-heavy pastoral kitsch, dried-flower overload, gingham / farmhouse tropes
- Costume countryside fantasy instead of a real urban literary apartment
- Excessive “whimsy,” mushrooms, cottage props, or fairy-tale styling
- Soft-focus romance that erases modern editorial restraint
- Slow living as costume rather than as an adult reading ritual

## Do

- Shoot and select frames that could belong to a real afternoon
- Keep French atelier restraint and UWS apartment energy dominant
- Let imperfection in surface, light, and placement read as human

## Do not

- Chase trends, filters, or viral visual gimmicks
- Over-prop, over-grade, or over-produce
- Open on black cards or bury the subject under text
