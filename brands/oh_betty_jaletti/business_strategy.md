# Business Strategy — Oh Betty Jaletti

Business constraints for every BettyOS workflow. Creative must serve these facts.

## Primary objective (current)

**Grow a qualified waitlist** for First Edition: The Reading Hour.

Qualified means desire for the ritual and the collection — not discount hunters or accidental clicks.

## Inventory

**No inventory is currently available.**

Content must build desire without pretending product is in stock. Do not use shop-now language, fake carts, or “available today” claims.

## Current launch window

**Not yet recorded as a firm date range in BettyOS brand sources.**

Until a launch window is written here explicitly:

- Speak in honest waitlist terms already used by the brand (“coming soon,” “dropping soon,” First Edition members get first access)
- **Do not invent** ship dates, drop days, or countdowns

## Product and bundle pricing targets

**Not yet recorded in BettyOS brand sources.**

Until prices are written here explicitly:

- Do not invent retail prices, bundle totals, discounts, or “worth $X” claims
- Sell the feeling and the waitlist — not a fabricated offer stack

## Content’s job right now

1. Make The Reading Hour feel recognized and specific
2. Introduce the First Edition collection without fake availability
3. Invite the right people onto the waitlist
4. Protect brand trust for when inventory actually exists

## Do

- Prioritize waitlist growth in plan, repurpose, and future review decisions
- Prefer CTAs: “Join the waitlist” / first-access framing
- Keep claims aligned with products.md and voice.md

## Do not

- Optimize for vanity engagement that dilutes waitlist quality
- Run scarcity theater or false urgency
- Imply stock, shipping, or purchase paths that do not exist
- Invent pricing, promotions, or launch calendars
