# Identity — Oh Betty Jaletti

Operating guidance for BettyOS. Load before planning, writing, or reviewing content.

## What this brand is

Oh Betty Jaletti is a **literary lifestyle brand**.

Its First Edition is **The Reading Hour** — objects made for the ritual of reading, not for trend performance.

## Brand mood mix

Use this mix as the creative north star:

| Share | Reference |
|------:|-----------|
| 40% | Upper West Side literary apartment |
| 20% | French atelier restraint |
| 20% | English countryside |
| 20% | Modern editorial restraint |

**American heart** sits under the restraint: warm, human, and sincere — never cold luxury, never costume heritage.

## Character of the brand

- Refined, nostalgic, human, warm, and visually intentional
- Cinematic without being glossy or over-produced
- Slow living that stays adult and editorial — never cottagecore fantasy
- Quiet confidence; the ritual already exists; the brand names and honors it

## Do

- Treat The Reading Hour as a real, familiar ritual — not a product gimmick
- Favor lived-in literary atmosphere over styled sets
- Keep French atelier restraint and Upper West Side apartment energy primary

## Do not

- Position as a generic lifestyle, home-decor, or “cozy aesthetic” brand
- Drift into cottagecore, farmhouse pastiche, or costume nostalgia
- Make the brand feel staged, template-made, or obviously AI-generated
- Invent origin stories, heritage claims, or manufacturing narratives
