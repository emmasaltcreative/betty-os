# Oh Betty Jaletti — Content Package
### First Edition: The Reading Hour | Waitlist Growth Campaign

---

## Campaign Direction

**Goal**
Grow a qualified waitlist for First Edition: The Reading Hour by making the reading ritual feel recognized, specific, and worth waiting for.

**Audience**
People who already protect their reading hour — who notice the glass beside them, the light in the room, the moment before the first page. They are on Instagram and Pinterest. They are not looking to be sold something. They are looking to be seen in something they already do.

**Central Creative Idea**
*The hour already belongs to you.*

Every piece of content names a moment inside the reading ritual — not a product announcement, not a launch countdown. The reading hour is real. It happens today. The First Edition exists to honor it. The waitlist is the invitation to be among the first when it arrives.

This idea lets us work honestly: no inventory, no false urgency, no staged sets. The assets we have — hands turning pages, warm lamp light, a drink at the edge of the frame — are the ritual. We name what they already are.

**Recommended Publishing Rhythm**

| Week | Platform | Pieces |
|------|----------|--------|
| Week 1 | Instagram Reels | Piece 1 (priority) |
| Week 1 | Pinterest | Piece 4 (static pin) |
| Week 2 | Instagram Feed | Piece 2 |
| Week 2 | Pinterest | Piece 5 (static pin) |
| Week 3 | Instagram Reels | Piece 3 |

Space pieces at least 3–4 days apart. Pinterest pieces can go live independently of Instagram cadence — pin them as soon as editing is complete.

**Why it fits Oh Betty Jaletti**

Careful editing can surface the warmth and intimacy the brand is built on. This piece will reach the broadest new audience because the evening-lamp aesthetic has wide discovery reach on Reels — the brand voice in caption and hook does the filtering work to attract the right audience.
