# Oh Betty Jaletti — Production Plan
**Session:** July 24, 2026 | 30 min | Home + Restaurant (6PM EST)

---

## 1. Session Objective

Build qualified waitlist demand by capturing the *feeling* of The Reading Hour — not a product, but a moment. Today's content should make someone feel like they've been missing a ritual they didn't know they wanted.

---

## 2. Recommended Creative Concept

**"The Hour Before Dinner"**

The idea: there's a thin slice of time — before the table fills up, before the noise starts — that belongs entirely to you. A book. A drink. A candle. This session captures that feeling across two settings: the quiet of home and the warm hum of a restaurant at early evening. Kids present is not a liability; the contrast of *your* private reading world against the beautiful chaos of family life is actually on-brand and human.

No products are needed. You are the product today.

---

## 5. Styling and Prop Notes

- **Book:** Something with a beautiful spine — classics, essay collections, or anything with letterpress/textured cover. Avoid airport paperbacks.
- **Glass:** Wine, sparkling water with a lemon, or a cocktail — whatever you're actually having. It should look real.
- **Your outfit:** Keep it simple and considered. A linen shirt, a silk blouse, or a well-worn sweater. Nothing trendy or logo-heavy.
- **Surface at home:** Clear 80% of what's there. Leave 1–2 intentional objects. Less is more.
- **Kids:** If they appear, let it be candid. Do not pose them.

---

## 6. Lighting and Composition Direction

**At home:**
- Shoot near a window. Overcast light is ideal — soft, directionless.
- If it's bright, use a sheer curtain or face the window (don't shoot into it).
- Warm up with a lamp in the background if available — it adds depth.

**At the restaurant (6PM):**
- Golden hour or early restaurant ambient light will be flattering and atmospheric.
- Avoid overhead restaurant lighting directly above — find a booth or window seat.
- Turn off your flash. Grain is fine. It reads as editorial.

**Composition rules for today:**
- Leave room at the top for overlay text (Pinterest).
- Shoot vertical (4:5 or 9:16) unless it's a detail shot.
- Off-center subjects. Negative space is intentional, not empty.
