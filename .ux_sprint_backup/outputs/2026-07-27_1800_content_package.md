# Oh Betty Jaletti — Content Package
### Campaign: The Reading Hour | First Edition Waitlist

---

## Campaign Direction

**Goal:** Build qualified waitlist demand for Oh Betty Jaletti's First Edition, *The Reading Hour*, before inventory is available.

**Audience:** Readers who treat their reading time as sacred — women in their late 20s to early 40s who curate their home environments with intention, spend money on beautiful objects that mean something, and feel vaguely unsatisfied by generic book merch. They're on Pinterest saving apartment aesthetics. They're on Instagram following small literary brands. They care about *how* they read, not just *what* they read.

**Central Creative Idea:** *The Reading Hour already exists in you.* The brand doesn't invent the ritual — it honors one that's already happening. This campaign positions Oh Betty Jaletti as the name for something readers have always felt but never had a word for: that specific, irreplaceable quality of a real reading hour.

**Recommended Publishing Rhythm:**

| Day | Platform | Piece |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Pinterest | Piece #1 — Static/slow mood pin |
| Day 2 | Instagram Reels | Piece #2 — The ritual reel |
| Day 3 | Email/Waitlist | Piece #3 — Brand story email with embedded video |
| Day 5 | Instagram + Pinterest | Piece #4 — Page-turn loop |
| Day 7 | TikTok / Instagram Reels | Piece #5 — POV waitlist closer |

**Editing Notes:** ⚠️ *Requires light editing.* Trim to the most atmospheric portion of the clip (woman settled at desk, lamp glowing, book open). ### Piece #2 — "The Ritual Reel"
**Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Objective:** Mid-funnel emotional storytelling. Show the texture of a real reading hour to earn the follow and the waitlist sign-up.

**Exact Asset File Paths:**

- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2547.mov` (secondary — page turn with drink in frame)

3. `IMG_2549.mov` — Pull out to the full room: woman, lamp, atmosphere. Closing text and CTA.

**On-Screen Hook** (appears over Shot 1, fades in after 1 second):
> *You've been doing this your whole life.*

**Supporting On-Screen Text:**
- Over Shot 2: *The reading hour. You already know what it is.*
- Over Shot 3: *Oh Betty Jaletti — made for it. First Edition, coming soon.*

**Caption:**
> There's a specific kind of evening that belongs entirely to you.
>
> A book you've been saving. A drink that's just right. The lamp on low. Nothing that needs doing.
>
> Oh Betty Jaletti is a literary lifestyle brand built around that hour — the one most people rush past.
>
> The Reading Hour is our First Edition. Products designed to make that time feel like it was always meant to be yours.
>
> Waitlist is open. Link in bio.

**CTA:** "Join the waitlist — link in bio" (on-screen + caption)

**Suggested Duration/Format:** 25–35 seconds. Vertical. Paced slow and deliberate — this is not a fast-cut reel. Think editorial, not trend-chasing.

**Editing Notes:** ⚠️ *Requires editing.* Cut the three clips together with gentle, slow transitions (dissolve or straight cut with no flash). Reduce the playback speed on `IMG_2545.mov` by 15–20% to add weight to the page-turn moment. Grade all clips to a consistent warm, slightly desaturated tone — the goal is continuity between the bright natural light of clips 1 and 2 and the lamp-lit warmth of clip 3. Use a single clean serif font for all on-screen text. No bold. No emoji in overlays. Ambient or minimal classical/acoustic audio — avoid anything trending that will date the piece.

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:** This three-clip sequence moves from intimate detail (the hand, the rings, the page) to the full world of the brand (the woman, the room, the atmosphere). It's the brand's creative idea made visual: the reading hour is already happening — Oh Betty Jaletti is simply the name for it. The restraint in the editing approach mirrors the brand's modern editorial quality.

### Piece #3 — "The Brand Story Email" *(with embedded video)*
**Platform:** Email / Waitlist landing page
**Objective:** Convert curious visitors to committed waitlist signups. This is the deepest brand storytelling piece — for people who've already clicked through from social and want to understand what Oh Betty Jaletti actually is.

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2547.mov` (hero embed — overhead page turn with drink, warm natural light)

**Recommended Shot Order:**
Single embedded loop at the top of the email or landing page hero section. The clip plays silently, on loop, as the reader scrolls into the brand story copy.

**On-Screen Hook** (appears as HTML text above the embedded video, not burned into the clip):
> *For the hour that belongs to you.*

**Supporting On-Screen Text:** None burned into the video. Let it breathe as a silent visual. All storytelling lives in the email body copy.

**Email Subject Line Options:**
- *You already have a reading hour. We made something for it.*
- *First Edition — The Reading Hour*
- *This is what Oh Betty Jaletti is for*

**Email Body Copy (suggested):**
> There's a version of your evening you protect.
>
> Maybe it's after the kids are in bed. Maybe it's Sunday before anyone else is awake. Maybe it's the forty-five minutes on a train when you finally open the book you've been carrying around for two weeks.
>
> We call it the reading hour.
>
> It doesn't have to be an hour. It just has to be yours.
>
> Oh Betty Jaletti is a literary lifestyle brand. Our First Edition — *The Reading Hour* — is a small collection of objects built to honor that time. An heirloom book cover. A commonplace book. A reading candle. A glass worth drinking from.
>
> Nothing loud. Nothing branded for its own sake. Just beautiful things that make your reading hour feel like it was always supposed to be this way.
>
> The collection isn't available yet. But the waitlist is open, and First Edition members will have first access.
>
> *Join us.*
>
> [Join the Waitlist →]

**CTA:** Primary button — "Join the Waitlist" / Secondary text link — "Tell me more about the First Edition"

**Suggested Duration/Format:** Single-send welcome email or landing page hero. Video loops silently (no autoplay audio). Email should read in under 90 seconds.

**Editing Notes:** ⚠️ *Requires light editing.* Trim `IMG_2547.mov` to its smoothest 8–12 second loop — the moment where the hand rests on the open page with the drink visible in frame is the strongest composition. Export as a compressed MP4 or convert to GIF if email client compatibility is a concern. No text overlay, no color grade changes needed — the natural warm light of this clip is already ideal for a clean, editorial email aesthetic.

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:** This is the most conversion-oriented piece in the package, and `IMG_2547.mov` is its perfect visual anchor. The image is human, unhurried, and unmistakably literary — a hand on a book, a drink nearby, nothing performative. It communicates the brand's entire sensibility without a single word of copy visible in the frame. For a reader arriving on a waitlist page, this is exactly what earns trust.

### Piece #5 — "POV: You Found It"
**Platform:** TikTok + Instagram Reels
**Objective:** Bottom-of-funnel. This piece speaks directly to the reader who has been waiting for a brand like this without knowing it existed. Drive waitlist clicks.

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2545.mov` (opening — intimate, close, page turning)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2549.mov` (closing — full room, lamp glowing, complete world)

**Recommended Shot Order:**
1. `IMG_2545.mov` — Cold open. No music yet. Hands on pages. Rings. White sweater. Natural light.
2. `IMG_2549.mov` — Cut at the 4-second mark. Now we see the whole world: the room, the lamp, the woman, the atmosphere. Music or ambient sound comes in here.

**On-Screen Hook** (appears immediately, white text on the opening frame):
> *POV: someone finally made a brand for your reading hour.*

**Supporting On-Screen Text:**
- Over Shot 1: *You've been doing this your whole life.*
- Over Shot 2 (timed sequence, text fades in and out):
  - *The Reading Hour is the First Edition from Oh Betty Jaletti.*
  - *An heirloom book cover. A commonplace book. A reading candle. A glass.*
  - *Nothing generic. Nothing loud.*
  - *The waitlist is open.*

**Caption:**
> For the people who treat their reading hour like it matters. (Because it does.)
>
> Oh Betty Jaletti is a literary lifestyle brand. Our First Edition — The Reading Hour — is a small collection of objects for serious readers who care about the ritual, not just the book.
>
> We're not live yet. The waitlist is. Link in bio — First Edition access goes to the list first.
>
> Tell me in the comments: what does your reading hour look like? 👇

**CTA:** "Join the waitlist — link in bio" + comment prompt for algorithm engagement

**Suggested Duration/Format:** 20–30 seconds. Vertical. Paced slowly for the first half, slightly more direct in the second half as the product reveal builds. This is the one piece in the package that allows the products to be named explicitly.

**Editing Notes:** ⚠️ *Requires editing.* The tonal shift between the bright natural-light clip (`IMG_2545`) and the warm lamplight clip (`IMG_2549`) needs to be handled carefully — either color-grade them toward a shared middle ground, or use the tonal shift deliberately as a narrative beat (day reading → evening ritual). The text sequence in Shot 2 needs careful timing: each line should have 1.5–2 seconds on screen. Use a restrained, elegant font — nothing chunky or bold. Avoid trending audio that will undercut the brand's editorial positioning; consider a quiet, warm acoustic instrumental or near-silence with ambient room tone.

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:** This is the brand's direct address to its exact customer — and the POV format on TikTok and Reels is one of the highest-converting structures for waitlist and community-building content. The "someone finally made this" framing positions Oh Betty Jaletti not as a product launch but as a relief — the brand readers didn't know they were waiting for. That emotional positioning is precisely aligned with the campaign's central creative idea and the brand's overall tone.

## Missing Assets

The following shots would genuinely strengthen the package and are not currently available in the indexed library:

1. **A close-up of the Oh Betty Jaletti products** — specifically the Heirloom Book Cover, commonplace book, or reading candle in a styled, natural-light setting. These are referenced in copy (Piece #5 especially) but cannot yet be shown. Until inventory exists, text references to products are appropriate; once available, a product detail shot would transform Piece #5 into a much stronger conversion piece.

2. **A hands-on product interaction shot** — someone slipping a book into the Heirloom Book Cover, writing in the commonplace book, or lighting the reading candle. This is the single highest-value missing asset for waitlist conversion content.

3. **A natural-light establishing shot of a styled reading space** that foregrounds Oh Betty Jaletti objects specifically. The current assets evoke the brand's world beautifully but belong to a general reading aesthetic rather than the brand's own visual identity. One clean, intentional brand still or short clip would anchor all future content.

*Note: All five content pieces above can be produced and published using only the existing indexed assets. The missing shots above are recommended for the next production session to deepen conversion performance in Phase 2.*
