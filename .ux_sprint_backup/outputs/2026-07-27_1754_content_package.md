# Oh Betty Jaletti — Content Package
### First Edition: The Reading Hour | Waitlist Growth Campaign

---

## Campaign Direction

**Goal**
Drive qualified signups to the First Edition waitlist by positioning The Reading Hour as the product line for people who treat reading as a considered, personal ritual — not a hobby, but a way of living.

**Audience**
Women 28–45 who curate their homes, read intentionally, and are drawn to objects with meaning. They follow literary accounts, slow living creators, and independent lifestyle brands. They don't buy mass — they wait for the right thing.

**Central Creative Idea**
*"The hour is already yours. We're just making it more beautiful."*

This campaign doesn't sell products — it sells the feeling of the reading hour itself. Each piece invites the viewer into the ritual and ends with a quiet, confident prompt to claim their place on the waitlist. The tone is warm but never precious, literary but never academic, aspirational but lived-in.

**Recommended Publishing Rhythm**

| Week | Platform | Piece |
|------|----------|-------|
| Week 1 | Instagram Reels | Piece #1 — The Ritual Reel |
| Week 1 | Pinterest | Piece #2 — The Still Life Pin |
| Week 2 | Instagram Reels | Piece #3 — The Page Turn Reel |
| Week 2 | Pinterest | Repin / reshare Piece #1 to Pinterest feed |

Allow 3–5 days between posts. Do not stack same-week posts on the same platform.

### Piece 1 — *"This Is The Hour"*
**Priority Rank: 1**

**Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Objective:** Brand awareness → waitlist click. Introduce the emotional world of Oh Betty Jaletti through the reading ritual. Drive traffic to waitlist link in bio.

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2549.mov` (opening atmosphere — woman at desk, wrapped in blanket, evening lamp glow)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2547.mov` (mid-reel intimacy — overhead page turning with pink drink in frame)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2545.mov` (close — tight hand/page detail, clean and quiet)

**Recommended Shot Order:**
1. `IMG_2549.mov` — Open on the full scene. Woman reading at her desk, salt lamp glowing. Let it breathe for 2–3 seconds before text appears. Establishes the world.
2. `IMG_2547.mov` — Cut to overhead. Hands on pages, drink on the table. The ritual in its details. 3–4 seconds.
3. `IMG_2545.mov` — Final close-up. Just hands, pages, rings. Quiet and intentional. 2–3 seconds. Hold before CTA card.

**On-Screen Hook (first 2 seconds):**
> *"There's an hour in the day that belongs entirely to you."*

*(White serif type, lowercase, centered over `IMG_2549.mov` opening frame. Fade in at 0:01.)*

**Supporting On-Screen Text:**
- Over `IMG_2547.mov`: *"We built something for it."*
- Over `IMG_2545.mov`: *"The Reading Hour — First Edition"*
- Final card (black or deep neutral): *"Waitlist now open. Link in bio."*

**Caption:**
> There's an hour that's yours. Before the noise starts, after it ends, or carved quietly from the middle of the day. You already know the one.
>
> The Reading Hour is Oh Betty Jaletti's first collection — designed for people who treat that hour with intention. We're building it slowly and getting it right.
>
> First Edition waitlist is open. Link in bio if you'd like to be among the first. ✦

**CTA:** *Link in bio → waitlist signup page*

**Suggested Duration / Format:** 20–28 seconds | Vertical video | Reels

**Editing Notes:**
> ⚠️ **Requires editing.**
> - Trim each clip to specified durations.
> - Sequence in order listed above.
> - Apply a consistent warm color grade — slightly desaturate, pull highlights toward cream, keep shadows amber-toned. Do not over-filter; the lamp warmth in `IMG_2549.mov` should feel continuous with the daylight warmth in `IMG_2545.mov`.
> - Add text overlays in a clean serif font (recommend: Freight Text, Cormorant Garamond, or similar). White on dark frames, dark ink on bright frames.
> - No music required — if adding audio, choose something instrumental, quiet, and non-trending (trending audio will undercut the brand tone).
> - Final card: 2–3 seconds, centered type, no animation.

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
`IMG_2549.mov` provides the establishing "world" — atmospheric, warm, literary, Upper West Side meets English evening. The progression into `IMG_2547.mov` and `IMG_2545.mov` moves from scene to sensation, which mirrors the brand's editorial restraint philosophy. Three clips, three beats: *the world, the ritual, the object.* Nothing staged, nothing explained.

**On-Screen Hook:**

*(For video pin: fade-in text at 1 second over the clip. For static pin: text can live in the pin description only — keep the image clean.)*

> *"Join the waitlist"*

**Pin Description / Caption:**
> Some hours deserve more than good intentions. The Reading Hour is Oh Betty Jaletti's first collection — an edit of objects for people who read with care. Heirloom book covers, a commonplace book, a reading candle, and more. First Edition waitlist is open now.
>

**CTA:** Pin links directly to waitlist signup page.

**Suggested Duration / Format:**
- Video Pin: 10–15 second loop from `IMG_2547.mov`

**Editing Notes:**

> - For video pin: trim to 10–15 seconds, loop cleanly (ensure no jarring cut at loop point).

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
The overhead composition of `IMG_2547.mov` is the closest asset in the library to the brand's "French atelier meets Upper West Side" mood. The blush drink, the open novel, the ring detail — it's intimate without being domestic. On Pinterest, this image will perform in "reading aesthetic" and "slow living" search categories, attracting exactly the audience who will feel seen by the brand and motivated to join a waitlist for something intentional.

### Piece 3 — *"Some People Have a Reading Hour. You're About to Have a Reading Hour."*
**Priority Rank: 2**

**Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Objective:** Mid-funnel. For people who've seen the brand once and want to understand what it *is*. Slightly more explanatory than Piece 1 — introduces the product concept without hard-selling. Ends on waitlist CTA.

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2545.mov` (opening — clean, intimate, daylight page turn)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2547.mov` (middle — overhead with drink, relaxed and full of detail)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2549.mov` (close — full scene, woman at desk, atmospheric)

**Recommended Shot Order:**
1. `IMG_2545.mov` — Open tight. Just hands and pages in natural light. No context yet. 2 seconds before text appears.
2. `IMG_2547.mov` — Widen out. Overhead scene. Let the drink and the rings tell a small story about leisure and care. 3–4 seconds.
3. `IMG_2549.mov` — Full room. The woman, the lamp, the whole world. This is the reveal — the reading hour, fully inhabited. 3–4 seconds. Hold before final card.

*Note: This is the inverse order of Piece 1 — detail to world rather than world to detail. Creates a different emotional rhythm: discovery → context → belonging.*

**On-Screen Hook (first 2 seconds):**
> *"You already have a reading hour."*

*(Over the close-up of `IMG_2545.mov`. Quiet, conversational, personal.)*

**Supporting On-Screen Text:**
- Over `IMG_2547.mov`: *"You're just missing the objects that make it feel like one."*
- Over `IMG_2549.mov`: *"The Reading Hour — First Edition by Oh Betty Jaletti"*
- Final card: *"Heirloom book covers. A commonplace book. A reading candle. First Edition waitlist open now."*

**Caption:**
> Most people have a reading hour. A window of quiet carved from the day, a book they've been meaning to get back to, a drink that's gone slightly cold.
>
> We made Oh Betty Jaletti for that hour. Not to perform it — to live it a little more beautifully.
>
> The Reading Hour is our first collection. We're taking names.
>
> Waitlist link in bio. First Edition ships when it's ready and not before. ✦

**CTA:** *Link in bio → waitlist signup*

**Suggested Duration / Format:** 22–30 seconds | Vertical video | Instagram Reels

**Editing Notes:**
> ⚠️ **Requires editing.**
> - Reverse clip order compared to Piece 1 (tight → overhead → wide). This is intentional and must be followed for the emotional arc to work.
> - Same warm color grade as Piece 1 for visual consistency across campaign.
> - Text pacing: give each line room to land. Do not stack copy. One thought per cut.
> - Final card should list the four products explicitly (book cover, commonplace book, candle, glass) — this is the first piece in the series that names the products. Keep it minimal: centered, serif, no icons.
> - If this runs in the same week as Piece 1 on Instagram, use a different thumbnail/cover frame to distinguish the two in the grid.

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
This piece does the work of brand education without becoming a pitch. The reversed clip sequence (micro to macro) mirrors how the brand's audience discovers things — through a detail that earns their attention before the full picture is revealed. The caption voice — wry, warm, quietly confident — matches the Oh Betty Jaletti brand tone and distances the brand from generic lifestyle content. Naming the products for the first time in the final card rewards people who watch to the end.

## Missing Assets

The following shots would meaningfully strengthen this campaign and should be considered for the next production session:

**1. Product detail footage — Heirloom Book Cover**
A close-up of hands opening or holding the book cover — cloth or leather texture visible, ideally on a wooden surface or being lifted from a stack of books. This is the most-named product in the collection and has no visual representation in the current library. Even a 5-second clip would anchor future content.

**2. The commonplace book — a handwritten page or open spread**
A top-down shot of a pen resting on a handwritten page inside the commonplace book. Rings or a coffee cup in the corner for human scale. This single shot would significantly expand storytelling options.

**3. The reading candle — lit, close-up**
A tight clip of a lit candle with soft bokeh behind it. 5–8 seconds. This is one of the most shareable aesthetic assets in the slow living category and would perform well as a loop on Pinterest.

**4. A single clean product flat lay**
All four First Edition products arranged together on a neutral surface (linen, wood, or marble). No props, no hands — just the objects. This is the one asset that doesn't exist anywhere in the current library and would be essential for email, the waitlist page, and high-performing Pinterest static pins.

*All current campaign pieces (#1, #2, #3) can be completed with existing indexed assets. The above are recommendations for the next shoot or production session, not blockers for this package.*
