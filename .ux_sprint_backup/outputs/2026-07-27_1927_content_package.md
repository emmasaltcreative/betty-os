# Oh Betty Jaletti — Content Package
### First Edition: The Reading Hour | Waitlist Growth Campaign

---

## Campaign Direction

**Goal**
Grow a qualified waitlist for First Edition: The Reading Hour by making the reading ritual feel recognized, named, and worthy of anticipation — not by selling product that doesn't yet ship.

**Audience**
People who already treat reading time as a protected ritual. They notice the light in the room, the glass nearby, the particular quality of an unhurried hour. They are not looking for content about books — they are looking for content that sees *them*.

**Central Creative Idea**
*You already have the hour. We made objects that belong in it.*

The Reading Hour is not something Oh Betty Jaletti invented. It's something the audience already knows. The content job is to name that ritual with enough specificity that the right person feels seen — and curious about the First Edition as the natural companion to something they already do.

Every piece leads with the ritual. Products appear as quiet inhabitants of the scene, not the headline.

**Recommended Publishing Rhythm**

| Week | Platform Priority | Content Focus |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| Week 1 | Pinterest + Instagram | Establish ritual atmosphere; introduce First Edition softly |
| Week 1 | Email | Warm welcome / invitation to the right reader |
| Week 2 | TikTok + Instagram Reels | Tactile, unhurried moments; waitlist CTA |
| Week 2 | Pinterest | Evergreen static pin from existing footage |

Fewer pieces, held longer. This audience does not respond to volume. They respond to specificity repeated with restraint.

### Piece 1 — "The Hour Already Exists"

**Platform:** Instagram Reels + TikTok
**Objective:** Introduce Oh Betty Jaletti and the concept of The Reading Hour to the right audience; earn waitlist curiosity through recognition, not announcement.
**Format:** Short-form vertical video, 20–28 seconds

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2545.mov` (primary — hand turning pages, soft daylight, rings, white knit, clean light)
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2547.mov` (secondary — hand turning pages with pink drink in corner, warm and lived-in)

**Recommended Shot Order:**
1. Open on `IMG_2545.mov` — close overhead of hand resting on open book, natural daylight, quiet. Hold 4–6 seconds before any text appears.
2. Cut to `IMG_2547.mov` — same gesture, slightly warmer, the drink now visible in the upper corner. Holds the unhurried mood. 6–8 seconds.
3. Return to `IMG_2545.mov` for final text card moment — let the hand rest still on the page.

**On-Screen Hook** *(upper third, white serif, appears at ~2 seconds):*
> There's an hour you protect.

**Supporting On-Screen Text** *(appears in sequence, short lines, centered, unhurried):*
> You know the one.
> The book. The light. Whatever's in the glass.
> *We made objects for that hour.*
> First Edition — The Reading Hour
> Waitlist is open.

**Caption:**
> You already have the ritual. We just named it.
>
> The Reading Hour is Oh Betty Jaletti's First Edition — a small collection of objects made for the hour you protect. Not for trend. Not for a styled shelf. For the actual reading hour.
>
> First Edition members get first access when the collection is ready. The waitlist is open if that sounds like yours.
> *Link in bio.*

**CTA:** "Join the waitlist — link in bio."

**Duration:** 20–28 seconds

**Editing Notes — REQUIRES EDITING**
- Trim both clips to their quietest, most composed moments (avoid any abrupt motion at clip start/end)
- Use simple cuts only — no transitions, no effects
- Text overlays: white serif (Georgia or Times New Roman), soft drop shadow for legibility, centered, short line breaks
- Pace text to read slowly — one beat longer than feels necessary
- No music with lyrics; if music is used, choose something ambient and unhurried (soft strings or piano, low in the mix)
- Slight color warmth adjustment to ensure both clips feel continuous in tone
- Ratio: 9:16 native vertical — do not crop aggressively

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
`IMG_2545.mov` is the strongest asset in the library for the brand's core visual gesture — a hand on an open book, natural light, rings, white knit. It is lived-in, not styled. The overhead perspective is editorial without being cold. `IMG_2547.mov` adds the drink, which quietly introduces *The Good Glass* as a concept without naming it yet. Together they build the ritual atmosphere before any product language appears — the brand's exact approach. The hook "There's an hour you protect" is emotionally specific in the way the brand demands: not "book lovers" or "cozy vibes," but a particular, guarded hour the qualified audience immediately recognizes as theirs.

### Piece 2 — "The Waitlist Is Open" (Email)

**Platform:** Email (waitlist list — new subscribers or cold warm-up)
**Objective:** Convert curious visitors into qualified waitlist members by addressing one reader about a ritual they already have.
**Format:** Plain or lightly styled email, ~180 words, single CTA

**Exact Asset File Paths:**
- `assets/reading-hour-shoot/IMG_2545.mov` — export a single still frame (hand on open book, soft light) for use as the email hero image in 4:5 or 2:3 crop

**Recommended Shot Order / Image Use:**
- Hero image: still frame pulled from `IMG_2545.mov` at a moment when the hand rests quietly — not mid-turn. Crop to 4:5 or 600px wide for email rendering.
- No additional images needed. One image, unhurried.

**On-Screen Hook / Subject Line:**
> There's an hour you protect.

**Preview Text:**
> We made objects for it.

**Email Body:**

> You know the hour.
>
> The one where the book is open, the light is right, and the rest of the day has agreed to wait.
>
> Oh Betty Jaletti is a literary lifestyle brand. Our First Edition — *The Reading Hour* — is a small collection of objects made for that hour. The book cover you'll hold for years. The glass that belongs beside you. The candle that makes the room feel like the hour already started. The place to keep the lines worth keeping.
>
> Nothing ships yet. That's honest.
>
> But First Edition members get first access when it's ready — and that's the only reason to join.
>
> If the ritual resonates, the waitlist is open.

**CTA Button Text:** Join the First Edition Waitlist

**Editing Notes — REQUIRES LIGHT EDITING**
- Pull one clean still frame from `IMG_2545.mov` — hand resting (not turning) on open book
- Crop to 4:5 at approximately 600px wide for email hero
- No filter; apply only minor warmth consistency if needed
- Email layout: hero image → headline → body copy → single CTA button
- Font: brand serif for headline; clean readable sans or serif for body
- No countdown timers, no "limited spots" language

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
Email is where this brand can be most itself — unhurried, one voice to one reader. The body copy introduces all four First Edition objects (Heirloom Book Cover, The Good Glass, Reading Candle, Commonplace Book) without a feature list or price. "Nothing ships yet. That's honest." is the exact voice register — warm, undefensive, confident enough to tell the truth without apology. The still from `IMG_2545.mov` gives the email a hero image that feels observed, not art-directed. The CTA has no urgency theater. It earns the click by asking only the right people.

### Piece 3 — "The Reading Nook at Dusk" (Pinterest Static Pin)

**Supporting On-Screen Text** *(lower safe zone, small and restrained):*

> Objects for the ritual. Waitlist open.

**Pin Description (for Pinterest SEO + voice):**
> There's an hour that belongs to the book. The salt lamp low, the room quiet, the rest of the evening agreeing to wait. Oh Betty Jaletti's First Edition — The Reading Hour — is a small collection of objects made for exactly that hour. Joining the waitlist is the way in. No pressure, no countdown. Just first access when it's ready.
> *Oh Betty Jaletti — literary lifestyle brand.*

**CTA:** Pin links to waitlist landing page.

**Editing Notes — REQUIRES EDITING**
- Export one still frame from `IMG_2549.mov`; choose the most composed, least motion-blurred moment
- Minor warmth adjustment if needed — do not over-filter; the salt lamp glow is a visual asset, preserve it
- Add text overlays in white serif with soft shadow; keep copy minimal (two short lines maximum per zone)
- Final export: 2:3 ratio, minimum 1000px wide for Pinterest quality

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
Pinterest is an evergreen discovery engine for this exact audience — people who search "reading nook aesthetic," "slow living books," and "literary home." `IMG_2549.mov` has the most complete scene in the library: a person actually reading, warm lamp light, literary wall art, a cozy but not costume-y atmosphere. The salt lamp introduces warmth that reads evening ritual, not cottagecore — the mood sits closer to UWS apartment than pastoral fantasy. The still frame, cropped carefully, becomes a pin that can drive qualified waitlist traffic for months without paid support. The description uses Pinterest's natural language without abandoning brand voice.

*Note: The assets in `IMG_2549.mov` do lean slightly toward cottagecore territory (salt lamp, fringed blanket, wall posters). Frame selection should favor the desk, the book, and the woman's focused posture over any prop-heavy areas of the frame. Choose the most editorial moment.*

**On-Screen Hook** *(appears at ~12 seconds, not at the open — resist the urge to front-load):*

**Supporting On-Screen Text** *(final seconds, small, white serif):*
> Oh Betty Jaletti — First Edition
> Waitlist open. Link in bio.

**Caption:**
> The reading hour. You already know it.
>
> We made the First Edition for people who protect it.
> Four objects. No rush. First Edition access for those on the waitlist.
>
> Link in bio.

**CTA:** "Link in bio."

**Duration:** 15–18 seconds

**Editing Notes — REQUIRES EDITING**
- This is a slower, more minimal version of Piece 1 — do not reuse the same cut
- The key edit decision: delay all text until the final 4–5 seconds. Let the footage breathe.
- Fade between clips (≤ 0.25 seconds) rather than hard cut — this piece is slower in intention
- No music required; ambient room tone or very low-key instrumental only
- Text: smaller than Piece 1, more restrained — this is a mood piece, not a pitch piece

**Why It Fits Oh Betty Jaletti:**
This piece is the meditative counterpart to Piece 1. Where Piece 1 introduces and names, Piece 5 simply holds the moment. TikTok rewards hooks that arrive in the first second — but for a brand that rejects trend pressure, letting the footage breathe and delaying the text tests whether the *right* viewer stays. Those who stay through 12 seconds of a quiet hand on a book are the audience worth finding. The same two assets serve a different creative purpose without requiring new footage or a different shoot.
